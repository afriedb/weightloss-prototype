Why It Matters

Excess adiposity is a systemic stressor.

Chronic energy surplus can promote ectopic fat deposition, insulin resistance, low-grade inflammation, and endothelial dysfunction—pathways linked to cardiometabolic disease progression.

Insulin resistance cascade

Impaired glucose uptake drives compensatory hyperinsulinemia and metabolic strain.

Inflammatory signaling

Adipose tissue can release cytokines that elevate systemic inflammatory tone.

Cardiovascular remodeling

Excess weight is associated with hypertension, dyslipidemia, and vascular stress.

Sleep & respiratory load

Higher adiposity increases risk for sleep apnea and reduced sleep quality.

Clinically observed risk associations

Type 2 diabetes Coronary heart disease Stroke NAFLD Sleep apnea Osteoarthritis

Risk increases with degree and duration of excess body fat.
